# Autonomous Launch Intelligence

## Outcome

Turn a product launch question into a decision-ready brief in under ten minutes.

## Workflow

1. **Scout** searches public sources, customer language, competitor pages, and recent category signals.
2. **Atlas** clusters the evidence into the three strongest themes and writes a concise point of view.
3. **Prism** checks claims against the source set, flags weak evidence, and assigns confidence scores.
4. **Relay** converts the findings into launch recommendations, risks, and the next three actions.

## Human checkpoint

The operator reviews the evidence-backed brief before any recommendation is sent to a team or external system.

## Success metrics

- 18+ relevant sources per run
- 80%+ evidence confidence
- Less than 10 minutes end-to-end
- A brief with three themes, eight citations, and three recommended actions
